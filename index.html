Operation Guide & Nano Hardware Protection

                
            

            

                

                    
1. Actions on IC pins

                    
• Left-click on the dot: wire between an IC pin and a block.

                    
• Left-click on pin state button: toggle logic level 0 ↔ 1.

                    
• Right-click on IC pin: change role VCC → GND → GPIO.

                

                

                    
2. Actions on wires & blocks

                    
• Left-drag a wire segment: Move the wire and lock its position.

                    
• Right-click a wire or block: Delete immediately.

Wiring: Arduino Nano / Uno to ZIF-16 Socket

                
            

            

                

                    
                

                

                    
Socket: left column 1–8 (top to bottom), right column 16–9 (top to bottom).

                    
Nano / Uno: each socket position connects to the pin shown in its box.

                    
Power: the firmware drives VCC positions HIGH and GND positions LOW through these pins. Do not connect 5V to the socket.

                    
Ground: connect one Nano GND pin to the socket position marked GND.

                    
Leave unused positions unconnected.
